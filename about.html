Our Founding Story

From a Single Dog
to a Thriving Community

In 2010, Sarah Mitchell rescued an anxious border collie named Finley and quickly realized that the local options for force-free training were almost nonexistent. Determined to do better, she spent three years earning certifications in applied animal behavior, opened Pawfect Academy in her backyard, and never looked back.

What began as weekend classes for a handful of neighbors has grown into a full-service canine training center with 12 certified trainers, a 5,000 sq ft indoor facility and over 2,400 happy graduates. But the mission remains identical to that first session with Finley: help every dog and owner build a relationship worth celebrating.

Our Core Values

The Principles That
Guide Every Session

Evidence First

Every method we use is backed by peer-reviewed behavioral science. We never use techniques that haven't been proven to work and proven to be humane.

Partnership

Training isn't something we do to dogs — it's something we do with them. We cultivate willing cooperation, not reluctant compliance.

Continuous Growth

Our trainers attend conferences, earn additional certifications and stay current with the latest behavioral research to offer you the best methods available.

Compassion Always

Impatience and frustration have no place in our training sessions. We meet every dog exactly where they are and celebrate every small win.

Excellence

We hold our training to the highest possible standard — thorough assessments, detailed progress tracking and rigorous graduation criteria.

Community

Our graduates join a lifelong community. Alumni events, online forums, trainer Q&As and refresher classes keep you connected well beyond graduation.

Life at Pawfect

A Place Where Dogs
Always Come First

Walk into Pawfect Academy on any given morning and you'll smell treats, hear the sound of clickers and watch tail wags everywhere. This is the culture we've built intentionally.

World-Class Facility

5,000 sq ft climate-controlled training hall, outdoor agility area, sensory garden and puppy socialization pen — everything your dog needs to thrive.

Always Learning

We fund trainer education, host annual behavioral seminars and maintain a research library. Staying current isn't optional — it's our standard.

Community Give-Back

Free monthly workshops for rescue organizations, discounted training for shelter dogs and annual scholarships for families who need financial assistance.
