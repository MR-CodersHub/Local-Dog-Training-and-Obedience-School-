Quick Answers

Common Questions

How quickly can I start training?+

New cohorts start every 4–6 weeks. After your free consultation, we'll match you to the next available session that fits your schedule. Private sessions can often begin within 1 week.

Do you offer in-home training?+

Yes! Our behavior correction and private training packages can include in-home sessions for an additional fee. In-home training is particularly valuable for separation anxiety and resource guarding cases where the home environment is key.

Is parking available at your facility?+

Yes — we have a dedicated parking lot with 20 spaces, plus street parking on Training Lane. The facility is also accessible via the MAX Light Rail (Rose Quarter stop, 5-minute walk).

Can I bring my child to training sessions?+

Absolutely — we actually encourage it. Children (8+) are welcome in all group classes. We have a dedicated family training segment in our Puppy Foundation program and recommend involving all household members for the best results.
