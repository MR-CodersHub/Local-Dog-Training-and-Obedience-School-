From Reactive
to Remarkable

Meet Beau — a 2-year-old rescue border collie who arrived reactive, fearful and unable to walk on leash. After our 12-week Behavior Correction program, Beau is a confident, off-leash companion who runs agility courses on weekends.

Week 2
First calm walk on leash. No more pulling or reactivity to other dogs on the opposite side of the street.
Week 6

Program graduation — fully off-leash reliable, socialized and confident. Now enrolled in agility!
