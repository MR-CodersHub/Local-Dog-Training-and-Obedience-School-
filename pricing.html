Pricing FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes — all programs over $299 can be split into 2–3 interest-free monthly payments. Payment plan enrollment is available at checkout or contact us directly to set up your plan before enrolling.

Are there discounts for multiple dogs?+

Yes! Enroll a second dog from the same household in the same program cohort and receive 20% off the second enrollment. Contact us when enrolling to apply the discount.

What's your refund policy?+

Full refund if you cancel at least 7 days before the program start date. 50% refund if canceled 2–6 days before start. No refund for same-day cancellations or after the program has begun. Emergency exceptions are considered case-by-case.

Is there a scholarship or assistance program?+

Yes. We offer a limited number of need-based partial scholarships each quarter. We also partner with 6 local rescue organizations to provide discounted training for recently adopted dogs. Contact us to inquire about current availability.
