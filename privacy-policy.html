1 Overview

Pawfect Academy ("we," "us," "our") operates a local dog training and obedience school in Portland, Oregon, and this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you browse our site, contact us, create an account, enroll in a program, subscribe to our newsletter, or visit our facility — and what we do with it.

We follow a simple principle: collect only what we need to train your dog well and run our school safely. We never sell your personal information, and we never share it for third-party marketing.

Controller: Pawfect Academy, 123 Training Lane, Suite 200, Portland, OR 97201 • hello@pawfectacademy.com • (555) 123-4567

2 Data We Collect

a) Information you provide directly:

  • Contact & account details — name, email, phone number, password (stored hashed), address when you enroll or create a Pet Owner account via Signup / Login.
  • Dog & training profile — dog name, breed, age, weight, vaccination records, health or behavioral notes, training goals, and progress reports from your dashboard.
  • Enrollment & payment details — selected program (see Programs and Pricing), schedule preferences, and billing information processed securely by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Messages & feedback — anything you send through our Contact form, free-consult booking, newsletter signup, blog comments, or reviews.

b) Information collected automatically: device and browser type, pages visited, referring pages, approximate location (city level), and interactions with our site (e.g., video plays, filter clicks). This comes from standard server logs and privacy-respecting analytics.

3 How We Use Data

We use your information only for legitimate school purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries, book free consultations, and provide customer support.
  • To create and manage accounts, process enrollments and payments, and deliver training services.
  • To personalize training plans, track milestones, assign trainers, and ensure facility and dog safety.
  • To send transactional emails (confirmations, reminders, progress reports) and — only with your consent — our newsletter with training tips and offers. Unsubscribe anytime via the link in any email.
  • To improve our website, programs, and facility, and to comply with legal obligations (e.g., vaccination record keeping).

Legal bases where applicable (GDPR/UK-style regimes and US state laws): performance of a contract, your consent, our legitimate interests in operating a safe training school, and legal compliance.

4 Photos & Video Consent

Training sessions, graduations, and events may be photographed or filmed for progress tracking and, with your separate opt-in consent, for marketing (website, social media, Blog). You can withdraw photo consent at any time by emailing hello@pawfectacademy.com, and we will stop using new images of you or your dog going forward.

Class progress photos shared privately in your dashboard are never used publicly without explicit permission.

5 Cookies & Analytics

We use a small number of cookies and local storage for essential functions: remembering your theme (light/dark), text direction, login session, dashboard preferences, and form progress. With your consent, we may use anonymized analytics to understand which programs and articles are most helpful.

  • Strictly necessary: required for security, load balancing, and remembering settings. Always on.
  • Preferences: theme, RTL toggle, and recently viewed programs.
  • Analytics (optional): aggregated, non-identifying page statistics. You can block these in your browser without breaking core features.

We do not use cross-site advertising trackers and do not sell data collected via cookies.

6 Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only when necessary to run the school:

  • Service providers — secure hosting, email delivery, payment processing, and scheduling tools, bound by confidentiality and data-processing agreements.
  • Trainers & staff — only the details needed to deliver your dog's training and keep classes safe.
  • Veterinary or emergency contacts — if your dog needs urgent care during a session and you cannot be reached.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of people and animals.

We never sell personal information and never share it with data brokers.

7 Data Security & Retention

We protect data with HTTPS encryption in transit, hashed passwords, role-based access controls (separate Pet Owner and Admin portals), and regular access reviews. No method is 100% secure, so please use a strong unique password and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

We keep account and enrollment records for as long as your account is active and for up to 3 years afterward for progress history, warranties (lifetime refresher support), and legal record-keeping. Vaccination and incident records are kept as required by Oregon regulations. Newsletter data is deleted promptly after you unsubscribe.

8 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (including California, Oregon, and other US states with privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, opt out of marketing, and appeal a decision. To exercise any right, email hello@pawfectacademy.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We respond within 30 days (sooner where the law requires).

  • Update your profile, dog details, and communication preferences anytime in your dashboard.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing in one click — transactional safety emails (e.g., class changes) will still be sent while enrolled.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to records we must retain by law.

9 Children & Pets

Our programs are purchased by adults (18+). Children may attend classes with a parent or guardian, but we do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13 online. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. Pet health data you share is used solely for training and safety.

10 Changes & Contact

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. Material changes will be announced on this page with a new "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, by email or dashboard notice. Continued use of our services after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about privacy? Contact our team at hello@pawfectacademy.com, (555) 123-4567, or 123 Training Lane, Suite 200, Portland, OR 97201. You can also reach us via the Contact page.
