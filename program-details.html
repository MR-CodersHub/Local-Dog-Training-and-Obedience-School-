Week-by-Week Curriculum

What You'll Learn
Every Session

1
Foundation & Focus Week 1
  • Name recognition & eye contact
  • Marker training introduction
  • Handling & touch tolerance
  • Calm greeting behavior
2
Sit, Down & Stay Week 2
  • Solid sit with hand signal
  • Down from sit & stand
  • Duration stay (5–30 seconds)
  • Impulse control basics
3
Recall & Leave It Week 3
  • Reliable "come" command
  • Emergency recall foundation
  • "Leave it" with objects & food
  • Attention work with distractions
4
Leash Manners Week 4
  • Loose leash walking introduction
  • Direction changes & pace
  • Passing other dogs calmly
  • Proper equipment guidance
5–6
Polishing & Proofing Weeks 5–6
  • Adding distance & duration to all commands
  • Proofing with mild distractions
  • "Off" for jumping up
  • Door & threshold manners
7–8
Real-World Application & Graduation Weeks 7–8
  • Public environment training
  • Stranger greeting manners
  • Owner practice assessments
  • Graduation ceremony & certificate
