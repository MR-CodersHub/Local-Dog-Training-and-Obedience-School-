Common Questions

Program FAQ

How do I know which program is right for my dog? +

Book a free 20-minute consultation call. We'll ask about your dog's age, history, current challenges and your goals — then recommend the best program fit. If you're unsure after that, we offer a complimentary 15-minute in-person assessment.

What if my dog misses a class? +

Life happens. Each enrolled student gets one make-up session per program at no extra charge. All session notes and video resources are uploaded to your dashboard the same day, so you never fall behind.

Are your training methods safe for all dogs? +

Absolutely. We use 100% force-free, positive reinforcement methods — no shock collars, prong collars or aversive tools of any kind. Our methods are endorsed by the IAABC, APDT and Fear Free organization.

Can I join mid-program if I missed the start? +

New program cohorts start every 4–6 weeks, so there's always an upcoming session. We don't recommend joining mid-program as the curriculum is progressive, but you can join our waiting list and we'll notify you as soon as a new cohort opens.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes! We offer interest-free payment plans for all programs over $299. You can split any program into 2–3 monthly payments at checkout. We also have a scholarship program for qualifying families — contact us to learn more.
