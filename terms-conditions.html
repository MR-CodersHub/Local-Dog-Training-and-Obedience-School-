1 Our Agreement

These Terms & Conditions ("Terms") form a binding agreement between you ("client," "owner") and Pawfect Academy, 123 Training Lane, Suite 200, Portland, OR 97201 ("Academy," "we") whenever you use this website, create an account, or enroll a dog in any program — including Puppy Foundation, Basic Obedience, Advanced Off-Leash, Behavior & Reactivity, and specialty workshops listed on our Programs and Pricing pages.

By enrolling, booking a free consultation via our Contact page, or clicking "I agree" on Signup, you confirm you are at least 18 years old, you are the dog's owner or authorized caretaker, and you accept these Terms and our Privacy Policy.

2 Services & Eligibility

  • Force-free training only: all instruction uses science-based, reward-based methods. Prong, choke, shock/e-collars, and physical corrections are prohibited in our facility and classes (see Section 6).
  • Who may enroll: puppies must be at least 8 weeks old; group classes require age-appropriate vaccinations (Section 5). Dogs with a bite history must disclose it at enrollment and start with a private assessment.
  • Trainer assignment: meet our faculty on the Trainers page. We assign the best-fit certified trainer and may reassign for scheduling or behavioral needs.
  • Results: we guarantee professional, good-faith instruction aligned with our curriculum — not a specific behavioral outcome, which also depends on practice at home, health, and temperament.

3 Enrollment & Payment

  • Booking: enrollment is confirmed once payment is received and you get a confirmation email with schedule, location, and preparation checklist.
  • Pricing: current fees are on the Pricing page. Multi-week programs must be paid in full before the first session unless a payment plan is agreed in writing.
  • Late arrival & missed classes: sessions start on time. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may count as a missed session for safety reasons. One make-up session per program is offered when you notify us at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Lifetime support: graduates receive free refresher Q&As and pack walks as described at enrollment, subject to availability and good standing.
Private behavior programs require an intake questionnaire and vet records before the first 1-on-1 session so your behaviorist can prepare a safe plan.

5 Health, Vaccines & Safety

  • Vaccinations: dogs must be current on rabies (if age-eligible), DHPP, and Bordetella per your vet's schedule. Proof is required before the first group session.
  • Illness & parasites: dogs showing coughing, vomiting, diarrhea, or fleas/ticks must stay home; contact us for a make-up plan and vet clearance before returning.
  • Leashes & equipment: use a standard 4–6 ft leash and flat collar or harness. Retractable leashes are not permitted in class.
  • Facility rules: follow trainer instructions, keep dogs leashed unless directed, supervise children at all times, and clean up after your dog. Aggressive behavior toward people or dogs may require removal for everyone's safety (with a private-training alternative offered).

6 Conduct & Force-Free Rules

To protect every dog's welfare, the following are strictly prohibited at Pawfect Academy and may result in immediate dismissal without refund:

  • Use of aversive tools (prong, choke, shock collars) or physical punishment, hitting, or intimidation.
  • Harassment of trainers, staff, or fellow clients — in person, by phone, or online.
  • Bringing an undisclosed aggressive dog into group class without prior assessment.
  • Recording other clients or their dogs without consent.

We coach the whole family so cues stay consistent at home. If homework isn't working, tell your trainer — we will adjust the plan rather than increase pressure on the dog.

  • You remain responsible for your dog's behavior and for any injury or damage your dog causes to people, animals, or property.
  • To the maximum extent permitted by Oregon law, the Academy's liability is limited to the fees paid for the program giving rise to the claim. We are not liable for indirect or consequential damages.
  • Nothing in these Terms limits liability that cannot legally be limited, nor affects your statutory consumer rights.
  • If your dog injures another participant, you agree to share vaccination and insurance details promptly and cooperate in good faith.
Emergency care: if your dog needs urgent veterinary care and you cannot be reached, you authorize us to seek reasonable emergency treatment at your expense.
