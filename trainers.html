Meet the Head Trainer

The Face Behind
Pawfect Academy

Head Trainer & Founder

Sarah Mitchell

CPDT-KA · Certified Applied Animal Behaviorist

Sarah founded Pawfect Academy in 2010 after rescuing her border collie Finley and discovering a complete lack of force-free training options in the city. Twelve years and 1,200 personal clients later, she leads a team of 12 certified trainers who share her exact philosophy: trust builds everything.

Sarah specializes in puppy foundation, behavior modification for anxious and reactive dogs, and trainer mentorship. She lectures at the International Association of Animal Behavior Consultants annual conference and co-authored the "Pacific Northwest Force-Free Guide."
